## Instructions for Use

Review each question and answer YES or NO based on your organization's needs. Questions answered "YES" will be prioritized for implementation and fine-tuning during the development process. Questions answered "NO" may be deprioritized or excluded to streamline the platform for your specific use case.

**Note:** Some features may have dependencies on others or may require additional infrastructure, resources, or third-party services. The development team will assess technical feasibility during implementation planning.
