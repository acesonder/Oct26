## Introduction

Welcome to OUTSINC, a comprehensive outreach and case management platform designed with trauma-informed care principles. This platform serves clients, outreach workers, service providers, and administrators in their journey toward wellness and support.

### Key Features
- 🔒 **Secure & Private** - All data is encrypted and protected
- 🎯 **Goal-Oriented** - Track progress and achieve milestones
- 💬 **Communication** - Secure messaging between users
- 📊 **Comprehensive** - Complete case management tools
- ♿ **Accessible** - Built with accessibility in mind

## Getting Started

### Registration

**Screenshot**: `screenshots/auth/registration.png`

#### How to Register:

1. **Navigate to Registration Page**
   - Visit the OUTSINC platform URL
   - Click "Create Account" or "Register"

2. **Choose Your Role**
   - **Client**: Seeking support services (instant activation)
   - **Outreach Worker**: Field workers and case managers (requires admin approval)
   - **Service Provider**: Organizations offering services (requires admin approval)

3. **Fill in Your Information**
   - First Name and Last Name
   - Email Address (must be valid)
   - Username (this will be your unique identifier)
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm Password

4. **Submit Registration**
   - Click "Create Account"
   - **Clients**: Automatically logged in
   - **Workers/Providers**: Wait for admin approval

**Important**: Write down your username! You'll need it for future logins.

---

### Login

**Screenshot**: `screenshots/auth/login.png`

#### How to Login:

1. **Enter Credentials**
   - Username or Email
   - Password

2. **Submit**
   - Click "Login"
   - You'll be redirected to your dashboard

#### Password Reset:

**Screenshot**: `screenshots/auth/password-reset.png`

If you forgot your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Follow the link and create a new password

### First-Time Setup

**Screenshot**: `screenshots/common/welcome-tour.png`

#### Welcome Tour:

When you first log in, you'll see the **Welcome Tour**:

1. **Username Reminder**
   - The tour will display your unique username

2. **Feature Highlights**

   - Navigate through each feature with "Next" button
   - Use "Back" to review previous steps

3. **Tour Controls**
   - **Skip Tour**: Button in top-right corner
   - **Next/Back**: Navigate between steps
   - **Finish**: Complete the tour

4. **Restart Tour Later**
   - Go to Settings → Welcome Tour
   - Click "Restart Welcome Tour"

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes! All data is encrypted, passwords are hashed, and we follow security best practices.

**Q: Can I access OUTSINC on mobile?**
A: Yes! The platform is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and computers.

**Q: Who can see my information?**
A: It depends on your role. Clients' journals are completely private. Case workers can see case-related information. Admins have broader access for management purposes.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes, contact an administrator to request account deletion.

### Client Questions

**Q: Are my journal entries private?**
A: Yes! Your journal is 100% private. Only you can see your entries.

**Q: How do I find services near me?**
A: Use the Services Directory and filter by location. You can search by address or postal code.

**Q: Can I message my case worker?**
A: Yes! Use the messaging system to communicate with your assigned worker.

### Worker Questions

**Q: How do I approve a new client?**
A: Clients are auto-approved. Workers and service providers require admin approval.

**Q: Can I assign cases to other workers?**
A: Yes, through the case management interface, you can reassign cases.

**Q: What if I forget to complete a safety check-in?**
A: The system will send alerts. Contact your supervisor immediately if you missed a check-in.

### Admin Questions

**Q: How do I approve new workers?**
A: Go to Admin Dashboard → Approvals → Review pending applications.

**Q: Can I see all user activity?**
A: Yes, through the Audit Logs section, you can view all system activities.

**Q: How do I create database backups?**
A: Use System Health → Maintenance Tools → Backup Database.
